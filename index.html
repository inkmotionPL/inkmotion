Ask me

Questions

What is ink wash painting?

It's an old Asian style of painting with black ink and water. Each brush stroke is done once and can't be fixed, so every painting is unique.

What is pebble art?

Small pictures made from real sea pebbles glued onto paper or wood, then framed. Each one uses different stones, so no two are the same.
